Bulk Orders

Copies of the book and the workbook for every family you serve.

If you want the partners in your practice walking into the birth room ready, this is the easiest way to get it done. Order copies of Go Boil the Water, the Before the Room workbook, or both, and they ship straight to you. Hand them out at intake, tuck them into welcome baskets, or keep a stack on the shelf.

Where we can ship

Printed copies ship to the United States, Canada, Brazil, the United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Poland, Australia, New Zealand and Japan.

Somewhere else? The Practice Print License gets you the workbook as a PDF with permission to print up to 25 copies for your families, so you can print locally and skip the shipping. For 50 copies or more, pick "Somewhere else" below and request a quote.
