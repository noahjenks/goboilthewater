Before the Room

A Partner's Workbook for Supporting Homebirth

Go Boil the Water tells you what your job is. This is where you do the work. One chapter at a time, it walks alongside the book with a page just for you, a conversation to have with her, and questions to take to your midwife.

Start with Chapter 1. It's free.

How the workbook works

Every chapter of Before the Room lines up with a chapter of Go Boil the Water, and every one has the same four parts.

His page is for you alone. Nobody has to read it, so write the real answer and not the one that sounds good.

Together is for the two of you. Sit down with her, ask the questions, and write both answers side by side. Listen more than you talk.

Ask your midwife is a page to bring to your next prenatal visit. Ask every question out loud and write down what she says.

This week is one thing to do before you turn the page.

What's in the back

The last section holds the pages you'll reach for in the final weeks: your birth rules, who's in the room and who's on call, a transfer plan you hope you never use, the last week, your golden hour, and a few pages for after the baby comes. Fill them in as you go, and most of the thinking is done before labor starts.

Do I need to finish the book first?

No. Read a chapter of Go Boil the Water, then do the matching chapter here. If she's thirty-six weeks along and you're just now picking it up, go straight to Chapter 5 and the pages at the back, then come back for the rest.
